## 💡 About

PreWorkout helps you stay on top of your nutrition and fitness goals. Track your daily calorie intake, monitor your weight, calculate your calorie needs, and get personalized advice from an AI assistant — all in one place.

> 🌍 Available in **English** and **Polish** · 📱 Works on desktop and mobile

---

## ✨ Features

|     | Feature                | Description                                                                               |
| --- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 📊  | **Dashboard**          | Real-time charts for weight, calories, and protein trends at a glance                     |
| 📓  | **Diary**              | Track daily calorie and macro intake — inline editing, sorting, multi-day management      |
| 🧮  | **Calorie Calculator** | TDEE calculator based on your profile and activity level with deficit targets             |
| 🤖  | **AI Assistant**       | Chat with Gemini or Grok for personalized nutrition advice — context-aware with streaming |
| 🥤  | **Drinks Tracker**     | Log energy drinks, filter by category and sugar, view consumption heatmaps                |
| 📅  | **Calendar**           | Visual overview of logged days — spot gaps and stay consistent                            |
| ⚙️  | **Settings**           | Profile, preferences, and data import/export                                              |
| 🔐  | **Auth**               | One-tap Google Sign-In with secure per-user data                                          |

### 🤖 AI Assistant in detail

- **Two models** — Switch between Gemini and Grok on the fly
- **Auto-fallback** — Seamlessly switches provider if one is unavailable
- **Context-aware** — Tailored to your profile and goals
- **Diary attachment** — Share your data with the AI for deeper analysis
- **Streaming** — Responses appear in real-time
- **Flexible** — Use as a full page or from the sidebar
